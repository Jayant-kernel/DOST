# B experience workstream publication

User-designated B scope: browser voice controls, capture/playback/transcription client helpers, preference controls, accessible layouts, staff voice comparisons, blinded listening UI/session validation, analysis tooling and existing fictional sample assets. Core conversation behavior and provider/model settings are inherited from A's preceding 30 commits.

The 30 publication groups organize existing files by responsibility. They do not establish separate historical development dates or independently prove a 40% authorship share. The publisher must verify B's authenticated GitHub account and authorized commit identity before committing/pushing. No account impersonation, backdating or invented collaborator credit is needed.

No private recordings, profiles, databases, credentials, listener responses or model weights are included. The fifteen bundled WAVs are existing fixed synthetic fixtures with a manifest, not new provider requests. Preview/study serving paths were adjusted to the bundled fixture directory. The original source project and its local data remain untouched. The video editor project and real care-home footage are separate assets and are not imported.

Legacy dashboard/telephony/media pages outside the active browser conversation were not pulled into this package. Existing safety/privacy/quality and dependency warnings still apply. Saved-audio technical integrity is distinct from pronunciation, warmth or human preference.
